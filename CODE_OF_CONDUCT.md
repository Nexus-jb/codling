# Code of Conduct

We want Codling to be a welcoming project for everyone who uses it or works on
it.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, including when you disagree.
- Assume good intent, and give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for users and for the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sharing pirated software or links to it in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break
these rules, and may block people who do so repeatedly. To report a problem,
contact the maintainers privately through the Nexus-jb organization on GitHub.
